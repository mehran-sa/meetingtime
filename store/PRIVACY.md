# Meeting Tracker privacy policy

Meeting Tracker is an internal browser extension for tracking meeting links.

**What it reads.** When you click the extension button or one of its right-click menu entries, it reads the visible text and link addresses of the current tab to find a meeting's date, time, contact email and join link. It does not read any page at any other time.

**What it stores.** The meetings you save (brand, partner name, contact email, date, time, join link and the address of the page it came from) are stored in the extension's storage in your browser, on your computer. They are not encrypted. Meetings are deleted automatically a week after they end, and "Delete all meetings" in Settings removes everything at once.

**What it sends.** Nothing. The extension makes no network requests and has no server. If Chrome sync is turned on, Chrome itself syncs the extension's settings and brand representative names through your Google account; saved meetings are not synced.

**Contact.** Questions go to the team that maintains the tool.
